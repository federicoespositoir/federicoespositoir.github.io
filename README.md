# Federico Esposito

MA in International Affairs, John Cabot University, Rome.

I build datasets, statistical models and maps to study Italian politics and public policy. I work mainly in R and Excel.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/federicoespositoir/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/federicoespositoir)

## Projects

### [MA Thesis: Data Modelling and Data Management](https://federicoespositoir.github.io/ma-thesis-data-modelling/)

Political support across Italian provinces in four elections, 2018 to 2024. I built 14 provincial datasets from 13 official data series and estimated 304 cross-sectional regression models in R. The project includes the Excel datasets, regression tables, coefficient plots, static and interactive maps, and the R code.

### [Policy Brief: Inflation and Democratic Participation](https://federicoespositoir.github.io/policy-brief-inflation/)

How the 2022 inflation surge affected democratic participation, with the Italian general election of September 2022 as a case study. A policy writing sample for International Political Economy.

### [Interactive Maps: The 2024 European Election in Italy](https://federicoespositoir.github.io/interactive-maps-italy/)

Interactive maps of turnout and the populist radical right vote in the 2024 European election, for Italian provinces and for the municipalities of the Metropolitan City of Rome. Built in R with leaflet.

### [Podcast: Growing Up During the ISIS Era](https://federicoespositoir.github.io/podcast-growing-up-isis-era/)

A 26-minute episode on how my generation in Europe first met the Middle East through fear, and what changed once I studied its history and international relations. Written, recorded and edited for International Relations of the Middle East.
